# Tokenized asset model

1. **Description:** what the token is. Name, ticker, description, website, logo, inception date, ISIN, CUSIP and other identifiers.
2. **Underlying:** what it tracks or holds. Reference asset and ticker, reference ISIN and ratio, asset class, sector, region, denomination, reserves, custody, peg maintenance, S&P rating.
3. **Token mechanics:** how the token works and how value reaches the holder. Chain, contract, standard, decimals, native/bridged/wrapper, transfer limits, rebasing, income distribution, NAV accrual, admin permissions, oracles, staking.
4. **Legal claim and rights:** what you legally own. Tokenization structure, sponsorship, holder rights, fork rights, voting, dividend eligibility, seniority, bankruptcy remoteness, segregation, deposit insurance.
5. **Issuer and regulation:** who issues it and under what rules. Issuer, legal entity, LEI, domicile, jurisdiction, regulator, framework, dispute resolution, transparency page.
6. **Service providers:** who runs it. Platform, manager, custodians, brokers, auditor, administrator, paying agent, transparency method.
7. **Primary market and fees:** how you get in and out directly, and what it costs. Eligible investors, KYC, base assets, subscription and redemption terms, lockup, minimums, all fees.
8. **On-chain activity:** how it's held and used. Supply, holders, concentration, mints, burns, transfers, active addresses.
9. **Value and return:** what it's worth and what it has earned. Price, NAV, total and market value, APY, returns.
10. **Governance and security:** who controls it and what has gone wrong. Governance model, audits, bug bounties, incidents.
