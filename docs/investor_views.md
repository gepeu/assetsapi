# Investor views

A view answers one investor question through one lens. The questions draw on the ten axes in [tokenized_asset_model.md](tokenized_asset_model.md).

## Questions

1. **Exposure:** what am I betting on? Draws on Description and Underlying.
2. **Return:** what do I earn, how does it reach me, and what does it cost? Draws on Value and return, Token mechanics, and Primary market and fees.
3. **Risk:** what can go wrong, and how much could I lose? Draws on Value and return, Underlying, Legal claim and rights, Service providers, Issuer and regulation, Governance and security, and Token mechanics.
4. **Ownership:** what do I own, and what rights does it give me? Draws on Legal claim and rights, and Issuer and regulation.
5. **Access:** can I buy it, where and how? Draws on Primary market and fees, Token mechanics, and Issuer and regulation.
6. **Exit:** can I get out, how fast and near what price? Draws on Primary market and fees, On-chain activity, and Value and return. Liquidity, including holder concentration, belongs here rather than in Risk.

## Risk types

1. **Market:** the price falls.
2. **Peg and backing:** the token drifts from its value or isn't fully backed.
3. **Counterparty:** the issuer, custodian or broker fails.
4. **Smart contract and control:** a hack, a bridge failure, or an admin freezing tokens.
5. **Regulatory:** rules change or the asset loses its status.

## Lenses

1. **Now:** where this token stands today.
2. **Over time:** how it has moved.
3. **Against peers:** how it compares with similar tokens.
